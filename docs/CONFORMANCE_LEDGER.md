# The Conformance Ledger — Z App (z_core) ↔ zprivacy-lib

**Architecture (the owner, 13 Oct 2026, night — replaces the morning's migration plan).** Z App is our own product with its own private implementation; it does **not** migrate onto zprivacy-lib. zprivacy-lib is the SDK that puts Z inside other systems — the nodes of a future Z Network. The two are bound by **one Z Privacy Contract** (specification, golden vectors, security invariants), not by shared code: *two implementations are allowed; two contracts are forbidden.*

**Restore Authority — a contract invariant from today.** The node that holds the original is the only one that can reveal it. A token never implies remote restore authority.

**What this ledger holds.** One line per landed commit on z_core that touches scanner behaviour, token semantics, restoration, or session security: the commit, the area, what changed, and the answer — *contract* (both must conform), *app-specific* (Z App only), *library ahead* (the app imports it as a conformance improvement, by decision, never by dependency), or *open*. Held by the lead. Nothing is copied automatically.

Library repository: `YourVoicesApp/zprivacy-lib`. Its parity corpus is pinned to `d882c5f`; a later z_core behaviour is measured against a new corpus snapshot, never by rewriting that one.
