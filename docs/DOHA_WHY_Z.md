# Why Z — the Doha list

**The questions we expect in Doha, each answered in a way the asker does not
expect.** The owner started the list on 15 October 2026.

Every entry has the same shape:
- **the question** as it will be asked;
- **the answer** as a short chain, where each step is one sentence and the
  surprise comes first;
- **the facts** under each step, with sources;
- **what the answer may not say**, because an answer that cannot stand the next
  question in Doha costs more than it brings.

Slogan, final: «You know this. Every question to AI has a hidden cost: the
information you send with it.»

## Q1. What does Z protect?

**The answer, as a chain:**

1. **The surprise.**
   - AR: «في 2022 غرّمت الهيئة الإيرلندية لحماية البيانات شركة Meta ‏265 مليون يورو، بسبب بيانات نحو 533 مليون حساب، أي قرابة ريالين قطريين للحساب الواحد. ولم تكن الغرامة لأنها سُرقت، بل لأن تصميمها لم يحمِ البيانات.»
   - EN: «In 2022 Ireland's data regulator fined Meta €265 million over the data of about 533 million accounts. That is roughly two Qatari riyals an account. Meta was not fined because it was robbed. It was fined because its design did not protect.»
2. **The turn.**
   - AR: «اسمك وحده كلمة. اسمك في قائمة يصبح مستنداً ذا قيمة.»
   - EN: «Your name alone is a word. Your name in a list is a document with a price.»
3. **The principle.**
   - AR: «أمن المعلومات يبدأ من المكان الذي تضع فيه المعلومة.»
   - EN: «Information security starts where you put the information.»
4. **The close.**
   - AR: «الوقاية خير من العلاج. وZ هو الوقاية: يحجب اسمك قبل أن يغادر جهازك، وترى بعينك ما سيخرج قبل أن يخرج.»
   - EN: «Prevention beats cure. Z is the prevention: it hides your name before it leaves your device, and you see what will leave before it leaves.»

Oral short form: «اسمك وحده له قيمة. فلماذا نعطيه للـAI إذا كان لا يحتاجه؟»

**The facts under each step:**
- **Step 1.** On 25 November 2022 the Irish **Data Protection Commission (DPC)**
  fined Meta **€265 million**, and announced it on 28 November. The DPC is a
  regulator, **not a court**. The inquiry covered the Facebook data of about
  **533 million** accounts, scraped and then published. That is about €0.50 an
  account; at 4.16–4.22 QAR per euro (July–September 2026) it is about
  **2 QAR**. The infringement found was **GDPR Article 25(1) and 25(2): data
  protection by design and by default.** So the regulator punished a design
  that did not prevent. That is step 4's «prevention», as law.
- **Step 2.** What was scraped was **a list**: names together with phone
  numbers, dates of birth, places, e-mail addresses and relationship status.
  Each line on its own meant little; 533 million lines together were worth
  scraping, publishing and fining. That is step 2, measured by the case itself.
- **Step 3.** A question sent to an AI with a document in it **puts** the
  information somewhere else: on a server the asker does not control. Z acts
  at that moment, on the device, before the text leaves.

**What the answer may not say:**
- *«The Irish court.»* It was the regulator, the DPC.
- *«Your name is worth two riyals.»* The two riyals are a fine divided by a
  number of accounts, not a price.
- *«Meta leaked names.»* It leaked records in which the name was **one** field.
- *«Z never sends your name.»* Z finds what it can and shows it to you before
  anything leaves. It can miss: on 15 October the owner's own test missed the
  first name «Markus», and he protected it by hand.
