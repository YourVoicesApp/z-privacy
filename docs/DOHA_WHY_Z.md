# Why Z — the Doha list

The points we carry to Web Summit Qatar about why Z matters. The owner starts
the list on 15 October 2026. Each point says **what is said on stage**, **the
fact under it**, its **source**, and **what the claim may not say**, because a
point that cannot stand a question in Doha costs more than it brings.

Slogan, final: «You know this. Every question to AI has a hidden cost: the
information you send with it.»

---

## 1. Your name alone has value

**Said in Doha (oral):**

> اسمك وحده له قيمة. فلماذا نعطيه للـAI إذا كان لا يحتاجه؟
>
> Your name alone has value. So why give it to an AI that does not need it?

**The fact under it.** On 25 November 2022 the Irish Data Protection
Commission fined Meta **€265 million**. It announced the fine on 28 November
2022. The inquiry concerned the Facebook data of about **533 million**
accounts, which had been scraped and then published.

Divided by the number of accounts, that is about **€0.50 per account**. At
4.16–4.22 QAR per euro (July–September 2026), that is **about 2 Qatari riyals**.
This is a division, made only to show scale. It is **not** a legal price of a
name.

**The stronger half of the fact — and it is Z's own.** The fine was not for a
theft. It was for **GDPR Article 25(1) and 25(2): data protection by design and
by default.** The regulator found that the system did not protect the data **by
its design**. Z is that principle, made into a product: the protection is in
the design, and it is on before anyone asks for it.

**What the claim may not say:**
- *«Your name is worth two riyals.»* The two riyals are a division of a fine,
  not a price.
- *«Meta leaked names.»* The scraped data was names **together with** phone
  numbers, dates of birth, places, e-mail addresses and relationship status.
  The point stands because the name is the first line of every such record. It
  is not the only line.
- *«Z never sends your name.»* Z finds what it can and shows it to you before
  anything leaves. It can miss: on 15 October the owner's own test missed the
  first name «Markus», and he protected it by hand. The honest form is:
  **Z hides your name before it leaves your device, and you see what will leave
  before it leaves.**
