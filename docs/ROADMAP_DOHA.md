# The road to Doha — one number

**Web Summit Qatar, Doha, 31 Jan – 3 Feb 2027.** From 14 Oct 2026 that is **15 weeks**. ALPHA is $1,197; flight, hotel and visa are outside it; nothing is owed until it is paid.

**The owner's answer to «why do we go» (14 Oct):** Z is free, so nothing is sold to a user. What is sold is the thing itself. «تطبيق عمره أشهر ولديه مستخدمون بالألف — كم من المستثمرين يريد شراءه؟» So the road has one number on it: **users by Doha**, and everything else is in service of that number or is cut.

Today that number is **0**.

## The lead's reading of what is actually being sold

The app is free and keeps nothing, so it earns nothing and measures nothing. What a buyer buys is not the app's revenue; it is:

1. **zprivacy-lib — the SDK and the contract.** This is the asset. It is what another company can put *inside* its own system, and it is the only part that can be bought and used by someone who is not us. The architecture the owner ruled on 13 Oct says this already: the app is ours and stays ours; the library is how Z gets into other people's systems.
2. **The proof that it works in the world** — users, and the two hosts (V1-H, V1-I) that show Z inside a business and between two businesses.
3. **The team.** One founder, honest numbers.

So: **the app earns the users, and the users prove the library.** The pitch in Doha is not «buy our app»; it is «this many people already trust this boundary, and here is the library that puts it inside your product».

## The wall in front of the number, named plainly

**Z runs on Linux only.** A free application that thousands of people can install does not exist on Linux alone — almost nobody who needs Z has it. Windows is paper 074's work: the branch is merged, the CI is driven by hand, the Dart half is not run there.

**Nothing on this road matters more than a Windows build a normal person can install.** Not Arabic, not the search bar, not the exchange round. Users cannot exist without it.

**And counting them without touching them.** Z keeps nothing and sends nothing, so we cannot count people — and we will not start. What we can count honestly is **downloads from our own server**, which belongs to us and touches nobody. That is the number we quote, labelled as what it is: downloads, not active users. Anything stronger would be a claim we cannot hold, and the whole product is a promise about not holding things.

### Step 2 · A first Windows version, for the friend in Germany
Decided and made by the owner's own hand («نحن نقرر نسخة ويندوز ونصنعها»). It carries what is sealed, and no more.

**What is true and must be said with it:** the gate does not run the Dart half on Windows, so the glass is not measured there. This version goes to **one friend who is trying it**, not to people.

**Boundary:** installed on a Windows machine that is not the lead's, it opens, it reads a German letter, it protects, and a paste after Copy Protected carries no name. Not sent before that is seen.

### Step 3 · Publish, and count honestly
A download page, in German, with a Windows button and a Linux one, and a counter **on our own server**.

Z keeps nothing and sends nothing, so we cannot count people and will not start. What is counted is **downloads**, and that is what is quoted — never «users», which would be a claim the product itself forbids us.

**Boundary:** the owner downloads it from the site the way a stranger would, on a machine that is not his, and it works.

### Step 4 · The measuring campaign
Google search ads, **Germany only**, **$100–200 at most** (the owner). German privacy and legal keywords are among the dearest, so that money buys roughly 70–180 clicks, and perhaps 15–35 downloads.

So it is not a campaign; it is **a measurement**, and it comes out with one number: **what one download costs.** That number is the one an investor asks for, and the one that says whether a thousand is a road or a wish.

**Requires:** step 3 finished, or the money goes to a page with nothing to download. Germany also requires an **Impressum** by law — and ours is already on the German page: Faruk AB, 559473-3494, Hotellgatan 3, 311 31 Falkenberg. The German privacy page is there too.

**Boundary:** the cost of one download, written down.

What the stand shows: the app that people are already downloading, and the library that puts Z inside somebody else's product.

**Boundary — the owner's, not the lead's:** the full number, $1,197 plus flight and hotel, against what the counter says.

## What is cut from this road, and why

- **The Arabic reading base and the language packs.** The owner's own ruling of 14 Oct puts languages last; the base is a real defect, measured and written down (086), and it waits. Doha is Arabic-speaking, which argues for it — but a product nobody can install reaches nobody, in any language.
- **`.peak` and the closed document** (087): parked by the owner's word. It is the engine of spread *after* there are documents travelling, not before.
- **ODT, doc, xlsx, eml.** Three readers cover the office: PDF arrives, Word is written, text is pasted.
- **Everything in the libraries except V1-I.** The bindings, the wider parity, the numbers paper — all after Doha.
