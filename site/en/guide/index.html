How to use Z Privacy

One screen, from bringing a document in to keeping a protected copy of it. Every control this page names is quoted from the application’s own list of sentences, so this page and the program say the same words.

Bring something in

Z is one screen. At the top you bring in a document — PDF, Word or text — or write to the AI directly in the first part, with no document at all. Nothing leaves this screen by itself: «Only the protected version is sent to AI through Z Privacy», and «Your original data stays on this device unless you explicitly copy restored content».

Read the two columns

The document stands twice: «Original — local only» and «Safe — AI will receive». What an AI would receive stands beside the original, word for word, before anything is sent. The footer counts what the scan found: protected automatically, waiting for your word, normal.

Protect with one press

Press a word in the original and it is protected — the Safe column shows a token in its place, and the bar says «Protected as …». Press a protected word and «Undo protection» takes it back; nothing was learned from pressing — you protected it here, and Undo takes it back. Some words wait for your word rather than act alone: «Suggest: Z marks it and waits for your word», and the footer says how many wait.

Select with your hand

Drag across words to protect exactly what you chose — two clicks are one token, because a selection is your will. A hand selection never crosses a line break, and it never cuts into protected text: if it would, nothing changes and the bar says what it would have snapped to. In a table, press a value to protect the same cell in every held line.

Ask, and bring the answer back

Choose who answers in the middle; write your question. Your request rides in the Safe column with its values replaced. «Copy the chat» puts what would leave on your clipboard, protected — the question as an AI would receive it, and nothing earlier; paste it to any AI you use; bring what it said back with «Add an answer» and the names come back from your sessions — every name that chat has carried, not only the ones in what you copied. «The first answer opens the chat here. Every question after it carries the exchange before it — not the whole chat, and never the document again.»

What an in-app chat sends, said plainly, because it is what leaves this machine: your question is protected, and that protected question is the whole of what travels. Asked again inside Z Privacy it travels with one thing added — the exchange before it, the previous question and its answer in the protected form they left in — and never the document, which is sent once, with the question that first carried it. Nothing older is sent, and nothing older is lost: every earlier turn stays in your session’s own sealed file. What «Copy the chat» puts on your clipboard is that same protected question and nothing earlier, because the chat you keep with a model of your own keeps its own thread; an answer you bring back by hand still restores every name the chat has ever carried. What this costs you is said here too: a follow-up reaches a model that can no longer see the document, and no door here hands anybody the whole conversation.

Keep a copy — protected, or read back

«Save as PDF» asks what to call the document, then writes a protected copy named after your word and the session’s — marked «z-pdf · protected copy», with an added page that carries facts about the file and never a real value. The name is the one thing about that file that is not protected, and the app says so where you type it: «The file name stays exactly as you type it. It leaves with the file, so don’t put real names in it.» At the top of the screen, «z-pdf» opens such a file and restores the names your vault knows. That reading can be kept as well — «Save as a plain PDF» writes your own document with its names back in it: no added page, no footer, nothing that claims a protection, and the values in it in the clear.

Your vault

Everything protected is one block per value in your vault, on this device. The vault opens with your passphrase and locks with «Lock now».

If a second copy of Z writes while one is open, the next write here answers: «Another copy of Z Privacy changed this vault. Close that window, or lock and unlock here to take in its work.» — so a vault write is not lost in silence. What was measured is two copies of Z on one computer sharing one vault; two machines sharing a folder over a network are not, and neither is the file a session keeps, which has a guard and a measurement of its own.

The rooms

Settings holds the rooms by name: Language & rules, Vault & security, Sessions — and your identity card, where a list is a CSV with a «name» and a «type» column.

This page is in the language the application is in, and the application’s own Settings carries the line that opens it. One text in one place: there is no second copy of it inside the program to fall out of step with this one.
