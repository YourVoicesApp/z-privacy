Z Privacy

Use AI without moving sensitive information outside your device.

Before anything is sent, you are shown exactly what will leave your device — and nothing leaves without your word.

Linux build, in beta — not yet released

How it works zprivacy@mono-peak.com

The idea

The problem we are trying to solve is: how can individuals and companies benefit from AI and external services without having to move sensitive information outside their devices or organizations? For example, reviewing and verifying tax-related documents can take a significant amount of time, while AI can perform much of this work within minutes. The challenge is how to use AI without exposing clients’ names, personal information, or financial data.

Z Privacy works as an information vault inside the user’s computer. During the workflow, sensitive information is kept locally on the device and replaced with identifiers that Z Privacy can recognize and restore later. This allows the relevant content to be sent to an AI service without sending the original sensitive information. Before anything is sent, the user is shown exactly what will leave the device, and nothing leaves without their word. The interaction can take place either through a chat interface or through documents such as PDF files. When the AI response is received, Z Privacy restores the original information locally on the user’s device. Everything Z learns is kept in an encrypted vault on the user’s own machine — even the vault file on disk contains no readable name. Z Privacy is provider-independent and works with any AI service, including local models.

The path

Import→Scan→Review→Protect→Review what will leave→AI→Restore

As it appears in the app.

Z Privacy minimizes the sensitive content you send. It does not make you anonymous to the AI provider.

Privacy

  • Your originals stay on your device unless you explicitly export restored text.
  • The Vault is local.
  • Z Privacy has no account and no Z Privacy backend for your documents.
  • Clipboard contents are outside Z Privacy once copied.
